FAQ

        
Frequently asked
questions

      


      

        

          
Is this copying other creators?

          
No. The roadmap reveals strategy — format, cadence, and production approach — so you can adapt it to your own voice. It explicitly tells you to leave the identity and branding behind.

        

        

          
What does it cost?

          
CreatorRoadmap is free to use.

        

        

          
How many videos do I need?

          
We analyze the last 30 uploads and need at least 10 videos to identify a reliable content pattern.

        

        

          
Do I need AI tools to use this?

          
Not at all. The prompts recommend tools, but they work for fully human productions too. Use what fits your workflow.

        

        

          
Can I export the roadmap?

          
Yes — on any results page, use the Export button to download your roadmap as .txt, .md, or .json.
